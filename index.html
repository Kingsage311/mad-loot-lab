How the odds work

Each: every matching rule rolls independently.

One: one eligible rule is selected at random, then its chance is rolled.

Outfit rules only apply to matching zombies. Global rules apply to all outfits.

Chances are percentages. 0.1 = 0.1%. In-game multipliers affect the final odds.
